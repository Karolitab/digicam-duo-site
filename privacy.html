Digicam Duo

Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

In short

  • Digicam Duo collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • Your photos and videos stay on your phone. The app never sends them anywhere.
  • The app does not connect to the internet at all.
  • Apple handles the purchase. We never see your card or your Apple ID.

Who we are

Digicam Duo is made by Tim Gabe Holding Sociedad Limitada, a company in Spain. You can reach us at halopawapp@yahoo.com.

What the app uses on your phone

  • Camera. To show the live picture and to take your photos and videos.
  • Microphone. Only while you record a video, so the video has sound.
  • Photos. Only to add a photo or video to your library when you choose to save it. The app cannot see the rest of your library.

You can turn any of these off in your iPhone's Settings. Without the camera, the app cannot take pictures.

Where your photos and videos are kept

Everything you shoot is saved inside the app on your phone, like on a memory card. It leaves the app only when you save it to your library or share it yourself. Your camera settings, such as the date imprint and the brightness, are saved on your phone too.

What we receive

Nothing. The app has no server and makes no network connections. We do not receive your photos, your videos, your location, your contacts, a device ID or any record of how you use the app.

Apple may give us totals that cannot be linked to you, such as how many people bought the app or how often it crashed, if you agreed to share that with developers in your iPhone's Settings.

Payment

You pay once, through the App Store. Apple takes the payment under its own privacy policy. There are no subscriptions and no purchases inside the app.

What we never do

  • We do not show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • We do not sell or share data. We have none to sell.

Deleting your data

Delete a photo or video with the trash button in the app. Deleting the app removes everything it saved. Photos and videos you saved to your library stay there until you delete them in Photos.

Your rights

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have the right to see, correct or delete data a company holds about you. We hold none, but you can still write to us with any question. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Spain that is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

Children

Digicam Duo collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top.
