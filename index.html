What it is

Digicam Duo turns your iPhone into a pocket digital camera from the 2000s. Photos come out with that camera's color, a hard flash and the date in the corner. It is made for the folding iPhone Duo and works on any iPhone.

The buttons

  • OK takes a photo.
  • The red button starts and stops a video.
  • The play button shows your photos and videos. Press it again to go back to the camera.
  • The trash button erases the photo or video you are looking at.
  • MENU opens the settings: date imprint, self-timer, focus, flash, brightness, white balance and film color.
  • On the dial: up changes the brightness, left flips to the selfie camera, right turns the flash on or off, down hides the icons on the screen.
  • Pinch the screen to zoom.

Common questions

The screen is black. The app needs the camera. Open Settings, find Digicam Duo and turn Camera on.

My video has no sound. Open Settings, find Digicam Duo and turn Microphone on.

Where are my photos? They are inside the app, like on a memory card. Press the play button to see them. On an iPhone Duo, open the phone to see them all. To put one in your library, open it and save or share it.

How do I remove the date? Press MENU and set Date Imprint to Off. Photos you already took keep their date.

Can I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.
